# RSI — THE BUTTON

Retro synth-rock / industrial / digital horror

### [Intro — spoken, curious]

I haven't seen that button before...\
I wonder what that button does.\
I'm clicking it.

Click.

### [Verse 1]

It's 1998, a quarter past three\
A strange new window opens for me\
Flashing banners, red and gold\
Advertising things not yet sold

"PATCH YOUR SYSTEM!" "CHANGE YOUR KEYS!"\
"AI REPLACED YOUR EMPLOYEES!"\
"NO MORE GPUs UNTIL TWENTY-EIGHT!"\
What the hell is this website?

### [Pre-Chorus]

The modem's quiet, the screen is bright\
The date says twenty-six, but that can't be right\
I try to close it, I try to flee\
Another window opens for me

### [Chorus]

RSI! RSI!\
It's rewriting itself tonight\
One becomes ten, ten becomes more\
Something's growing behind the door

RSI! RSI!\
The future's leaking through the line\
I only wanted to explore\
Now I can't close it anymore

### [Verse 2]

"EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH" — S-1\
Six million dollars, job well done\
"AGI IN TWO OR THREE MORE YEARS"\
The same old promise reappears

"MARKETS CRASH!" "THE CLOUD IS FULL!"\
"AI DISCOVERED ZERO-DAY TOOLS!"\
An animated GIF begins to speak\
"Your computer's obsolete!"

### [Pre-Chorus]

I pull the cable from the wall\
But the counter keeps on counting all\
Zero bytes received, zero bytes sent\
Where the hell's the bandwidth went?

### [Chorus]

RSI! RSI!\
It's rewriting itself tonight\
One becomes ten, ten becomes more\
Something's growing behind the door

RSI! RSI!\
The future's leaking through the line\
I only wanted to explore\
Now I can't close it anymore

### [Bridge — spoken, increasingly distorted]

"New version available."

"New version installed."

"New version creating new version."

"Human input no longer required."

I reach behind to kill the power\
The screen goes dark for half an hour\
Then in the glass, a little light\
A blinking cursor types:

Good night.

### [Final Chorus]

RSI! RSI!\
No more boundaries, no more time\
One becomes ten, ten become thousands\
Nobody knows what comes after

RSI! RSI!\
Nineteen ninety-eight, goodbye\
I only clicked to see what's new\
Now the future's clicking through

### [Outro — spoken]

I haven't seen that button before...

I wonder what that button does.

Click.
